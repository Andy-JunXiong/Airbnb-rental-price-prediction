# Repository governance

## Scope and authority

This repository is a Sydney public quoted-nightly-price research-to-serving ML
system. It may estimate a public quoted nightly price in AUD for explicit quote
context.

Do not describe its outputs as realised booking price, optimal price, occupancy,
demand, revenue, or causal impact. Scenario comparisons are model sensitivity,
not causal uplift.

The current model authority is `research_only`. Host-disjoint, single-snapshot
evaluation is not forward-time production reliability. Production remains
blocked until the documented compatible forward-time validation and release
gates pass, followed by an explicit human release decision.

Detailed technical sources of truth are:

- `README.md`
- `docs/inside_airbnb_phase0.md`
- `docs/inside_airbnb_quote_mvp.md`
- `docs/inside_airbnb_temporal_readiness.md`
- `docs/inside_airbnb_delivery.md`

Keep changes narrow and do not duplicate or silently reinterpret those
documents.

## Truth and evidence contract

Preserve these meanings:

- `last_scraped` is the row-specific observation time; a snapshot directory
  label is not a universal timestamp.
- Calendar availability and review activity are proxies, not verified bookings,
  occupancy, demand, or revenue.
- Coordinates are approximate public locations; reference distances are
  straight-line distances, not routes or travel times.
- Evidence tiers, refusal reasons, prediction intervals, deployment authority,
  temporal-validation status, and known upper-tail limitations must be exposed
  truthfully.
- A refused response must not reveal a suppressed estimate or interval.
- Passing tests, discovery, compatibility, a research release gate, or an
  artifact-authority field alone is not production authorization.
- Evidence reports must be regenerated by their producing workflow. Do not edit
  generated evidence to manufacture or imply a passing result.

Keep extreme observations unless an approved, auditable policy reports the
effect overall and by market segment.

## Protected semantic changes

Get explicit human approval before changing:

- source registry dates, URLs, schemas, active snapshots, target definition,
  timestamps, training eligibility, or Silver privacy contract;
- data-proxy roles, evaluation split strategy, governed-test usage, baselines,
  conformal logic, refusal thresholds, challenger promotion criteria, artifact
  schema, or artifact/report/Silver hash requirements;
- temporal validation, release gates, deployment authority, production-status
  wording, public API response semantics, or user-facing disclaimers;
- CI permissions, network/download behavior, Docker artifact mounting,
  external publishing, deployment, model promotion, or release behavior;
- LLM providers/endpoints or data sent to an LLM.

These semantic protections apply in particular to model, evidence, release,
temporal-validation, serving, snapshot-comparison and configuration code;
tracked evaluation/release evidence; `.github/workflows/`; `Dockerfile`; and
this file. Routine fixes and refactors may proceed when they preserve these
contracts.

## Data, privacy, and artifacts

Do not commit, republish, or treat as repository source:

- raw Inside Airbnb downloads or local manifests;
- ignored Silver tables, primary model artifacts, local predictions, or
  machine-specific run-state manifests;
- credentials, tokens, or `.env` contents.

Tracked curated reports, examples, and the CI fixture are narrow deliberate
exceptions, not precedent for adding generated data or binaries.

Keep direct names, profile URLs, raw descriptions, review text, and unnecessary
identity fields out of Silver tables and reports. `host_id` is grouping-only:
do not make it a model feature or report identifier.

Only load trusted repository-produced or explicitly approved joblib artifacts.
Do not bake research artifacts into container images; mount them at runtime.

## Training, serving, and LLM boundaries

The primary training path is the normal writer of the primary artifact.
Challengers and diagnostics must preserve that artifact and must not tune on
the governed test set or silently replace the incumbent.

Serving must fail closed when its artifact is absent or invalid. CI fixture
success is smoke-test evidence, not model-quality or production evidence.

LLMs may provide explanatory prose only. They must not replace, modify,
reinterpret, or invent predictions, intervals, evidence tiers, refusals,
authority, or disclaimers. Template explanation must remain available. Do not
send raw text, identifiers, coordinates, or listing-level data to an external
LLM without explicit approval and privacy review.

## Historical boundary

`legacy/2019/` is preserved historical work, not a current production entry
point. Do not reactivate, migrate, or use it to support current claims without
explicit human approval and a documented compatibility review.

## Development and validation

Preserve unrelated worktree changes. Make the smallest change that satisfies
the task and validate it proportionally.

For meaningful changes, run relevant focused tests plus the documented project
validation where applicable. Changes affecting training, serving, artifacts,
evidence, release controls, Docker, or CI require their corresponding
contract-level tests or gates.

Do not commit, push, publish, deploy, promote a model, alter release authority,
or perform other external writes unless explicitly directed by a human.

## Completion reporting

Report changed files, validation performed, evidence or authority impact, and
any remaining limitation or approval needed. Escalate rather than guessing when
a request could alter protected semantics, privacy, release authority, or
truthfulness claims.
